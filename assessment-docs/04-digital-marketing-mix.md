# 04 — Digital Marketing Mix

## The 7P Digital Marketing Mix Applied to Start Strong

---

## Product

**What Start Strong offers:** Free, practical, beginner-focused running information.

The "product" is knowledge and guidance — delivered through:
- Beginner training plans and guides
- Educational blog articles
- Running gear advice
- Motivational content and habit-building strategies

**Value proposition:** Clear, honest running guidance for people who feel intimidated by conventional fitness content. No jargon, no false promises, no paid placements — just useful information.

**Content formats:**
- Long-form educational articles (750–2,000 words)
- Structured training plans with week-by-week progression
- Equipment comparison tables and checklists
- FAQ sections
- Proposed future: short-form video content (YouTube, Instagram Reels)

---

## Price

**Access model:** Completely free.

All content on Start Strong is accessible without subscription, registration or payment. This removes the conversion barrier and supports broad organic reach.

**Future monetisation options (proposed):**
- Affiliate commissions on running gear recommendations
- Display advertising (Google AdSense or similar) once traffic thresholds are met
- Sponsored content (clearly disclosed) with relevant brands

No monetisation is currently active. Any future revenue mechanism will be clearly disclosed to users.

---

## Place

**Primary distribution channel:** Google organic search.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages (publicly accessible, globally available, free). Content is distributed via:

| Channel | Role |
|---|---|
| Google Search | Primary traffic source; beginner search queries |
| Direct traffic | Bookmarks, return visitors |
| Social media | Traffic from Instagram, Facebook, YouTube (proposed) |
| Referral links | Coverage from other websites, forums |
| Email (future) | Newsletter for returning visitors |

The website is fully responsive — accessible on desktop, tablet and mobile. Mobile experience is prioritised as the majority of running-related searches occur on mobile devices.

**Social media (proposed):**
- Instagram: beginner running tips, motivational posts, blog promotion

- YouTube: how-to videos, beginner guides (proposed)

**Content marketing:**

- Evergreen content designed for long-term organic traffic

## People

As an independent content platform, the "people" element relates to:

**Content creators:** Editorial team responsible for accurate, honest, beginner-appropriate content.

**Audience relationship:** Visitor-facing tone is warm, practical and non-condescending. Content acknowledges that starting running is genuinely difficult and validates the reader's starting point.

**Trust signals:**
- Clear editorial standards published on the About page
- Health disclaimers on training content
- Transparent affiliate disclosure
- Privacy policy aligned with actual data collection practices

---

## Process

**Content production process:**
1. Identify search intent and audience need
2. Research topic using credible sources
3. Write content with factual accuracy and appropriate caveats
4. Review for tone, accessibility and SEO alignment
5. Publish via Markdown content collection
6. Monitor performance via Google Analytics

**User experience process:**
- Clear navigation with minimal friction
- Category filtering and search on the blog page
- Related article suggestions on every article
- Cookie consent before analytics activation

---

## Physical Evidence (Digital Equivalent)

In digital marketing, "physical evidence" relates to the credibility signals that build user trust.

Start Strong's credibility signals include:
- Professional visual design (consistent brand, typography, colours)
- Editorial standards page (About → Editorial Standards)
- Clear health disclaimers on training content
- Transparent affiliate disclosure
- Privacy policy aligned with actual practices
- Author attribution on all articles
- Internal links to credible supporting content
