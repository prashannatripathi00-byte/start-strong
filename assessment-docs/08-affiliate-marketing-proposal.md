# 08 — Affiliate Marketing Proposal

## Affiliate Marketing Overview

> **Current status:** No affiliate partnerships are active. This document outlines a proposed affiliate marketing strategy for future implementation. Affiliate links should not be added until a genuine partnership agreement exists.

---

## What Affiliate Marketing Is

Affiliate marketing involves earning a commission when a visitor clicks an affiliate link and makes a qualifying purchase from a partner retailer. The commission is typically a percentage of the sale value.

For Start Strong, affiliate marketing is a natural potential revenue stream because:
- The site already creates content about running gear
- Readers have genuine purchase intent when reading gear guides
- The content is editorially independent — no brand has paid for mentions
- Disclosure of affiliate relationships is straightforward and expected by readers

## Relevant Product Categories

| Category | Products | Affiliate Potential |
|---|---|---|
| Running shoes | Road shoes, trail shoes | High — high price point, frequent replacement |
| Running clothing | Tops, tights, socks | Medium — lower price, high volume |
| Running accessories | GPS watches, headphones | Medium-High — high price point |
| Recovery tools | Foam rollers, massage guns | Low-Medium — occasional purchase |
| Running apps | Strava, training apps | Low — mostly free or low-cost subscriptions |

## Potential Affiliate Programmes to Research

> These are programmes to investigate, not confirmed partnerships. Do not claim these partnerships exist.

- **Amazon Associates** — broadest product selection; lower commission rates
- **AWIN / Affiliate Future** — major UK/Irish affiliate network with running brands
- **Running shoe brand programmes** — many brands run direct affiliate programmes
- **Specialist sports retailers** — dedicated running shops often have affiliate programmes
- **Wiggle / ProBikeKit** — large cycling and running retailer with affiliate scheme

Before joining any programme, review:
- Commission rate and cookie duration
- Minimum payment threshold
- Editorial independence requirements
- Brand alignment with Start Strong values

## How Affiliate Content Would Work on Start Strong

### Current gear content (no affiliate links)

The Running Gear page and "How to Choose Running Shoes" article currently provide general, brand-neutral guidance. This is appropriate while no affiliate agreements exist.

### Future affiliate-enabled version

Once an affiliate agreement is in place:
1. Specific product recommendations can be added to gear articles
2. Affiliate links replace or supplement general links
3. Clear disclosure is added at the top of the page
4. Recommendations remain based on genuine merit — not commission rate

### Disclosure requirement

Every page containing affiliate links must include a prominent, plain-language disclosure. Example:

> *"Start Strong may earn a commission when you purchase products through links on this page. This does not affect the price you pay and does not influence our editorial recommendations."*

This disclosure must appear:
- At the top of the relevant page (not hidden at the bottom)
- Near individual affiliate links where practical
- In the site's Privacy Policy and About page

---

## Editorial Independence Principles

Accepting affiliate partnerships creates a potential conflict of interest. Start Strong's approach:

1. **Recommend products because they are good, not because they pay more commission.**
2. **Do not recommend products you would not recommend without the commission.**
3. **Disclose all affiliate relationships clearly and permanently.**
4. **Maintain the ability to remove a recommendation if a product's quality declines.**
5. **Never invent reviews, ratings or endorsements.**

User trust is the most valuable long-term asset of a content platform. Short-term affiliate revenue from misleading recommendations destroys trust permanently.

## What Not to Do

- Do not add fake affiliate links before an agreement exists
- Do not mark content as sponsored without a sponsorship agreement
- Do not recommend products you have not properly evaluated
- Do not hide or minimise affiliate disclosures
- Do not select products based only on commission rate
