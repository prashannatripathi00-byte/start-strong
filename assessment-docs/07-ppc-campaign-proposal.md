# 07 — PPC Campaign Proposal

## Proposed Google Ads Campaign — Start Strong

> **Important:** This is a proposed campaign plan. No paid advertising has been activated. All budget figures, performance estimates and metrics are illustrative proposals, not actual outcomes. Paid campaigns should not be launched without explicit approval and a confirmed budget.

---

## Campaign Objective

Drive targeted traffic from high-intent beginner runner queries to the Start Strong Start Running guide, with the goal of:
- Increasing organic brand awareness
- Growing return visitor traffic
- Supporting the overall content marketing funnel

---

## Target Audience

- Age: 18–45 (primary); 45–65 (secondary)
- Location: Ireland (primary); UK (secondary)
- Interests: Fitness, running, health, weight loss
- Intent signal: Searching for beginner running guidance or running shoe advice
- Device: Mobile (primary); desktop (secondary)

---

## Campaign Type

**Google Search Campaign**  
Keyword-targeted text ads shown to users actively searching for relevant terms.

## Conversion Tracking Plan

GA4 events to treat as conversion indicators:
- `cta_click` (Start Running button)
- `article_open` (blog article views)
- `article_engagement` at 75%+ scroll depth
- Session duration > 90 seconds

Note: As a free content site, there is no transactional conversion. Engagement depth is the primary quality signal.

---

## Evaluation Metrics

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Click-through rate (CTR) | > 3% |
| Bounce rate from ads | < 60% |
| Average engagement time | > 90 seconds |
| Cost per click (CPC) | < €0.80 |
| Engagement rate | > 40% |

---

## Potential Risks

- **Budget exhaustion without clear ROI:** As a non-commercial site, PPC ROI is difficult to quantify. Budget should be treated as a traffic experiment.
- **Quality Score impact:** Low relevance scores increase CPC. Ensure ad copy and landing pages are tightly aligned.
- **Click fraud:** Budget cap and geotargeting reduce this risk.
- **Ad fatigue:** Rotate ad copy variants after 2–3 weeks.

---

## Recommendation

PPC is most appropriate once:
1. The website is live and indexed
2. GA4 baseline data has been collected
3. SEO traffic has been assessed
4. A budget has been confirmed

Until these conditions are met, paid campaigns should not be activated.
